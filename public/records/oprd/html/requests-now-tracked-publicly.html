To the records officers of the Office of the Governor, Oregon State Police, and the Oregon Parks and Recreation Department:

The public records requests pending with your offices are now tracked on a single public page:

https://oprdvolunteerabuse.org/records-requests

For each office it states what was asked, what your office has said it holds, what is withheld and on what basis, and the statutory clocks running on both sides. It is updated as each of you acts. Every line is grounded in the correspondence, which is published
in full in the accountability registers.
