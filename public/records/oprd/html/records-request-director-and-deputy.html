This is a request under the Oregon Public Records Law. It is narrow by design and concerns two custodians only.

The period requested is February 1, 2025 to the present.

I request the following records concerning me, held by or sent or received by Director Lisa Sumption and Deputy Director J.R. Collier.

1. All communications concerning me sent or received by Director Lisa Sumption, in any medium, including email, text message, instant message, and any messaging application, on any device or account used for Department business.

2. All communications concerning me sent or received by Deputy Director J.R. Collier, on the same terms.

3. All communications between Director Sumption and Deputy Director Collier concerning me.

For the purpose of items 1 through 3, "concerning me" includes any communication referring to Robert White, Robert Samuel White, Robert S. White, Sam White, rsw@rswfire.com, oprdvolunteerabuse.org, rswfire.com, my date of birth, my volunteer service, my dismissal, my exclusion, my correspondence, my public records requests, the March 24, 2026 law enforcement contact at my residence, or the legal fund I have opened.

4. All notes, memoranda, summaries, or other records authored by either individual concerning me, including handwritten notes and calendar entries.

5. Any record reflecting a direction or instruction given by either individual concerning me, including any instruction regarding whether or how staff should respond to my correspondence.

This request does not seek records held by any other Department employee. If a communication responsive to items 1 through 3 was sent to or received from another employee, it is responsive because Director Sumption or Deputy Director Collier is a party to it.
