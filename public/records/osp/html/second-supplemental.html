I filed a Petition for Review of Denial of Public Records Request in this matter on July 22, 2026, and a first supplemental on July 24, 2026. I write to supplement the record a second time.

On July 27, 2026, five days after the petition was filed, Oregon State Police notified me that a thorough review has identified additional responsive records and that it is reopening PR27478, with production estimated at three weeks. I do not withdraw the petition.

single time-sensitive request in this matter — whether the March 24 dispatch recordings still exist and whether any responsive record has been destroyed, purged, or allowed to age off a retention schedule since April 2, 2026. That question can be answered
in a sentence and does not depend on the three-week review now underway.
