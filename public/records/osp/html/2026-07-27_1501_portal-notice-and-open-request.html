Oregon State Police has launched a new, streamlined process for submitting public records requests. Effective immediately, all requests must be submitted using our electronic request form. 

We are no longer accepting requests via email.  To submit a request, please use the following link: https://forms.osp.oregon.gov/forms/prrrecordsrequest

You can also access this form through our website at oregon.gov/osp under the “How do I request a public record” section.

This form should be used for both general public records requests and requests from public agencies.

Please allow at least 30 days for processing before following up on the status of your request.  For all other inquiries, we strive to respond to emails within 1–2 business days. 

Thank you for your cooperation as we work to improve our service.
