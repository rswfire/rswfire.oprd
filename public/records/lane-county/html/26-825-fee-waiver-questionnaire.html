Lane County may provide a fee waiver or reduction for people who otherwise would have to pay a fee for public records requests. Oregon law provides that a public body may provide a full or partial fee waiver for information provided in response to a public records request if fulfilling the request would serve the public interest by primarily benefiting the general public. Lane County considers all factors set out in the Attorney General's Public Records and Meetings Manual and relevant legal authorities in reviewing individual requests for fee waivers, including but not limited to:

* The extent of time, expense, and interference with the Department's regular business, including financial hardship on the County;

* The volume of the record(s) requested;

* The necessity to segregate exempt from non-exempt materials; and/or

* The extent to which the record requested does not further the public interest.

Please provide answers to the following questions to assist us in evaluating your fee waiver/reduction request. If your request contains sub-parts, respond to the questions below with respect to each sub-part, as appropriate.

* Provide general background/mission information on the requesting individual/group/organization as this information relates to public records requests from the County.

* Describe the purpose for which you intend to use the information/records requested.

* Describe your plans and ability to disseminate the information to the general public.

* Explain how your use of the records requested will benefit the general public.

* Explain any individual and/or private benefit derived from the disclosure of the records.

* Explain how disclosure of the records would serve a commercial purpose, if any.

* Provide any information that would help us evaluate your ability to pay any fees for fulfilling this request.

* Provide any other information you feel would be valuable in evaluating this fee reduction/waiver request.
