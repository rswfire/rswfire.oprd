Regarding your request for an explanation of the denial of your fee waiver:

ORS 192.324(5) allows public bodies to “…furnish copies without charge or at a substantially reduced fee if the custodian determines that the waiver or reduction of fees is in the public interest because making the record available primarily benefits the general public.” Lane County has reviewed your request for a fee waiver and has concluded that the disclosure of the records you have requested would not primarily benefit the general public. Therefore, your request for a fee waiver is denied. You may seek review of the County's determination pursuant to ORS 192.401, 192.411, 192.415, 192.418, 192.422, 192.427 and 192.431.&nbsp;
