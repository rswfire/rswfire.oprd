Background and mission of the requester. I am a private individual and the subject of the records I am requesting. I maintain a public accountability record that documents how government agencies
have handled, shared, and acted on my personal information. My requests exist to obtain the government's own documents and put them in front of the public.

Purpose for the records. To obtain the County's records about me, to learn what was disseminated to and by your Sheriff's Office, and to place those records on the public record.

Plans and ability to disseminate. Demonstrated, not promised. I already operate a public archive that publishes agency correspondence and records in full. The Oregon Parks and Recreation Department,
the Oregon State Police, the Department of Administrative Services, the United States Forest Service, the Oregon Legislature, and the Governor's office are already on it. As of today, so is this office.

Public benefit. The public has an interest in knowing when a federal agent sends a private citizen's date of birth, driver's license number, home, schedule, and private messages to a county sheriff's
office, and in how that office handled it once it had them. Disclosure lets the public see the government's conduct in the government's own words.

Private benefit. Yes. These are my own records and concern me directly. I am entitled to them on that basis alone, and my personal stake does not cancel the public interest in how they came to
exist.

Commercial purpose. None. I sell nothing. The archive is free to read and carries no advertising.

Ability to pay. I decline to turn my own records into a means test. The records concern me, the request serves the public interest, and no fee is warranted. I am not going to negotiate a price
for a file the County built about me.

Anything else. The last records custodian that treated a lawful request as a negotiation produced nothing but a fully documented public record of its own obstruction. Produce my records in full,
without cost, and without delay, and there is nothing to document.
